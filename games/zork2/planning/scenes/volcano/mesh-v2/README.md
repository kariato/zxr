# Selected untouched volcano reconstruction

Direct Tencent shape from the background-removed `concept-v2.png`: 287,036 vertices and 574,072 triangles, 10,334,084 bytes. `volcano-v2.glb` is unchanged pipeline output. `volcano-v2.blend` adds separate review cameras and lights without modifying imported geometry or UVs.

Front/back renders were inspected. Unlike the rejected first attempt, this has no rectangular background slab. The caldera cavity, two west ledges and side-room pods are recognizable. The rock detail is softened; ledge decks and the thin eastern shelf are partly fused into the walls. The chimney reads as a shallow groove rather than a verified continuous tube. Interior doorways, side rooms and safe landing clearance require manual editing. The rim is an incomplete cutaway, not a complete surrounding cone.

No cleanup, decimation, scaling or vertex movement. Native inference units; no runtime integration. Generation settings/hashes and Blender preservation checks are in the sibling JSON files. Tencent license and notice apply.
