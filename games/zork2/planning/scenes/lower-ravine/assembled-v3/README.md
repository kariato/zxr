# Jagged Ravine, revision 3

The ledge silhouette now has angular, irregular chips up to 30cm. Outer path edges remain aligned with the upper cliffs. Side cave passages extend 10m from their openings; the river-source passage extends 12m. Recessed rock surrounds at 2.8m and 6.2m provide visible depth cues. The river continues through its source tunnel.

Previous narrow curved ledges, 18–20m drop, continuous ground mapping and irregular upper cliffs remain. Width varies approximately 1.23–2.27m at chipped edges.

Use the Tunnel camera in the browser preview to inspect passage depth. Animated water is preview-specific; GLB water is static.
