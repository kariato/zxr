Front/back geometry renders were inspected. Seven room bowls, partitions and the workbench are recognizable, but the reconstruction is shallow and some arched doorways are closed recesses rather than open passages. Shelving and bench legs are softened/fused. The guarded room's north exit was omitted by the concept. These require manual editing before traversable integration; do not treat the render as proof of doorway clearance.
