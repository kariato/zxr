# Reviewed centered topiary scene

`garden-centered-topiary.blend` is the packed editable source for the game's modular garden. Six hedge sculptures occupy separate planted islands. The gazebo is 60% of its previous linear size and retains its brown roof, ivory framing and stone base. Models remain independently movable through their named `MOVE AND SCALE` roots.

`garden-centered-topiary-1080p.png` is the reviewed front view; `verification.json` records the six placements. Runtime packaging is handled by `tools/assets/garden-runtime.py`; the packaged GLB and its provenance are in `games/zork2/assets/scenes/underground-garden/`. Earlier local generation drafts are preserved separately.
