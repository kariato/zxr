# Well and Wonderland

One approach and vertical well with rooms at the top. Tea Room and Posts Room are two scales of the same space: shrinking makes the table's legs and underside become posts and a roof. Pool Room extends the small-scale view through the mouse-sized opening. Share room geometry, but keep carried objects, object visibility and engine identities independent of environmental scaling. The riddle door remains conditional.
